About Us

Last updated: October 2026

ConvertKit Lite is a free collection of online tools for everyday file jobs: merging, splitting and compressing PDFs, converting documents and images, extracting audio from video, and a few handy calculators. It is built and maintained by SAG, an independent developer.

What we believe

  • Tools should be free and work without creating an account.
  • Where possible, files are processed in your browser instead of being sent to a server.
  • Pages should be simple, fast and easy to use on a phone.
