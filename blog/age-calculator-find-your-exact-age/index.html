Age Calculator: Find Your Exact Age in Years, Months and Days

October 4, 20263 min read

How old are you, exactly? Not just in years, but in months, days, hours and even minutes. The Age Calculator from ConvertKit Lite works it out from your date of birth in a second. You can also find your age on any other date, past or future. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

What you'll learn in this guide

How to calculate your exact age, how to find your age on any date, how leap years are handled, and what the extra results mean.

What Does the Age Calculator Show?

After you enter your date of birth, the calculator gives you:

  • Your age in years, months and days.
  • Your age in hours and minutes, if you add your birth time.
  • Your total months, weeks, days and hours lived.
  • The day of the week you were born on.
  • How many days are left until your next birthday.

How to Calculate Your Exact Age

  1. Open the Age Calculator.
  2. Type your date of birth as day, month and year, or pick it from the calendar.
  3. Optional: choose your birth time to also get hours and minutes.
  4. Leave "Calculate age until" on today, or choose another date.
  5. Select Calculate Age.

How to Find Your Age on Any Date

The "Calculate age until" box lets you pick any date after your birth date. This is useful when you need to know:

  • How old you will be on a future date, such as a deadline or an event.
  • How old someone was on a past date, such as when a document was signed.
  • How old a child will be on the first day of school.

To go back to today, press Till now.

How Are Leap Years Handled?

The calculator uses real calendar dates, so leap years and months of different lengths are taken into account. You don't need to adjust anything yourself.

Do I Need My Birth Time?

No, the birth time is optional. If you leave it empty, you get your age in years, months and days. Add it if you also want hours and minutes. Results use your device's local date and time, so if you were born in another time zone, the hours and minutes may differ slightly.

Is My Date of Birth Private?

Yes. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so your date of birth is not uploaded to a server.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Swapping day and month. The fields are in day, month, year order.
  • Using a two-digit year. Enter all four digits, such as 1998.
  • Choosing an end date before your birth date. The end date must come after it.
  • Entering a date in the future. A date of birth can't be later than today.
