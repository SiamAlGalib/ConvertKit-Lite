Free PDF Tools With No Watermark, No Signup and Unlimited Use

October 11, 20264 min read

Most "free" PDF tools aren't really free. You upload a file, wait for the result, and then find a watermark on every page, a daily limit, or a popup asking you to sign up and pay. ConvertKit Lite was built to fix exactly that.

Here is what you get when you use ConvertKit Lite, and why it is different from the usual online PDF tools.

What Is ConvertKit Lite?

ConvertKit Lite is a free online toolkit for working with PDF files. You can merge, split, compress and convert documents right in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Open the site, pick a tool, and get your file.

Why People Choose ConvertKit Lite

1. No Watermark, Ever

Your finished file is clean. We never stamp a logo, a banner or a "processed by" line on your pages. What you download is ready to send, print or submit.

2. Unlimited Use

Merge ten files today and fifty tomorrow. There are no daily caps and no "you have used your free conversions" messages. Use the tools as often as you need.

3. No Signup, No Login

You do not need an email address or a password. There is no trial period and no credit card. Just open a tool and start.

4. Your Data Stays Private

Documents are often personal: contracts, invoices, ID copies, school papers. We treat them that way. Your files are used only to complete the task you asked for, and they are automatically deleted after processing. We do not read them, share them or sell them.

5. Fast Results

Most tasks finish in a few seconds. Drag your file in, press the button, and download the result.

6. Works on Any Device

ConvertKit Lite runs in the browser, so it works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iOS. Use it on your phone, tablet or computer without installing anything.

Tools You Can Use Right Now

  • Merge PDF: combine several PDF files into one document.
  • Split PDF: pull out the pages you need or break a large file into parts.
  • Compress PDF: reduce file size so it is easy to email or upload.
  • PDF to Word: turn a PDF into an editable document.
  • PDF to PowerPoint: convert pages into editable slides.
  • PDF to Excel: move tables out of a PDF and into a spreadsheet.
  • Word to PDF: save a Word document as a PDF.
  • PowerPoint to PDF: share your slides as a single PDF.

How to Use ConvertKit Lite

  1. Open the ConvertKit Lite homepage and choose a tool.
  2. Drag and drop your file, or click to select it.
  3. Adjust any options, such as the compression level.
  4. Click the button to process your file.
  5. Download the result. No watermark, no signup.

Free Tools vs. Typical Online PDF Tools

  • Watermarks: many tools add one on free plans. ConvertKit Lite never does.
  • Limits: many tools cap you at a few files per day. ConvertKit Lite has no daily limit.
  • Accounts: many tools force a signup. ConvertKit Lite needs none.
  • Cost: many tools hide features behind a paywall. ConvertKit Lite is free.

Tips for Better Results

  • Put your files in the right order before merging to save time.
  • Use a lower compression level if image quality matters more than file size.
  • Use a text-based PDF, not a scanned image, for the best PDF to Word results.
  • Keep a copy of your original file in case you want to try different settings.
