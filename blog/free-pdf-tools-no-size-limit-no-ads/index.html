Tips for working with large PDFs

  • Split first, then compress. Smaller parts are easier to handle and share.
  • Merge in the right order. Drag and arrange files before you hit merge.
  • Keep the original. Save your source file before compressing, in case you need full quality later.
  • Use a modern browser. Up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari gives the best speed.
