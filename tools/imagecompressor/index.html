Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite Image Compressor & Resizer.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs in your browser, so your images are not uploaded anywhere.

Which quality setting should I use?

70–85% usually looks nearly identical to the original while making the file much smaller. Go higher for detailed images, lower for the smallest size.

Which output format is smallest?

WebP is usually the smallest, followed by JPG. PNG is lossless, so quality does not apply and files stay larger.

Can I process many images at once?

Yes. Add as many images as you like, then download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.

Why is my result sometimes larger?

Flat graphics and screenshots can compress better in their original format. The result list marks any file that grew so you can keep the original.

Why can't my browser open a file?

Browsers can open JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF. Formats such as HEIC or HEIF may not work, so convert those to JPG first.
