Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite PDF to JPG tool.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. The images are created in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

How many images do I get?

One JPG for every page of the PDF, or just the first page if you choose that option. Several images can be downloaded together as a ZIP.

Which resolution should I choose?

72 DPI suits quick previews and web use. 150 DPI is a good all-round choice. 220 DPI is best for printing or zooming in on small text.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then convert the unlocked file.

Why are some pages very large files?

Pages with photos or dense graphics produce bigger JPGs. Lower the resolution or the quality to reduce the file size.
