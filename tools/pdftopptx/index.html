Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite PDF to PPTX tool.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. The PowerPoint file is created in your browser, so your PDFs stay on your device.

What is the difference between the two modes?

Exact look places each PDF page on a slide as a high-quality image, so the design is identical but cannot be edited. Editable text boxes places the text from the PDF as real, movable text boxes, but images, colors and shapes are not kept.

Will the slides be fully editable?

PDF files do not store slides, so a perfect editable copy is not possible. Editable text mode gives you text you can change, positioned as close to the original as the PDF allows. Pages with no text, such as scans, are added as images.

Why does the text look different in PowerPoint?

Fonts are matched to Arial, Times New Roman or Courier New. Spacing can shift slightly when the original font is not available. Use Exact look when the design matters most.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then add the file again.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several PDF files, convert them together, and download each PPTX or one ZIP.
