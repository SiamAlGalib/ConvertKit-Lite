About BMI

What the number means and what it does not.

How is BMI calculated?

For adults, BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared. This tool converts imperial measurements automatically.

Adult BMI categories

For adults aged 20 and older, commonly used screening categories are: underweight below 18.5, healthy weight 18.5–24.9, overweight 25–29.9, and obesity 30 or above.

Important note

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat and may not describe health accurately for every person. Adult categories are not intended for children or teens, whose BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific percentiles. If you have concerns about your weight or health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite BMI Calculator.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so your measurements are not uploaded to a server.

Can I mix metric and imperial units?

Yes. Weight and height units are chosen separately, for example pounds with centimetres.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat. It may not reflect health accurately for every person, such as athletes or older adults.

Can children and teens use this calculator?

No. The categories here are for adults aged 20 and older. For children and teens, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific percentiles.

What is the reference weight range?

It is the weight span that would give an adult BMI of 18.5–24.9 at the height you entered. It is a guide, not a personal target.
