How is BMI calculated?

For adults, BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared. This tool converts imperial measurements automatically.

Adult BMI categories

For adults aged 20 and older, commonly used screening categories are: underweight below 18.5, healthy weight 18.5–24.9, overweight 25–29.9, and obesity 30 or above.

Important note

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat and may not describe health accurately for every person. Adult categories are not intended for children or teens, whose BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific percentiles. If you have concerns about your weight or health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
