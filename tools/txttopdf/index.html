Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite TXT to PDF tool.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is created in your browser, so your text files stay on your device.

Can I select and search the text in the PDF?

Yes, for most files. Text in Latin-based languages is saved as real text. Files that use other scripts, such as Bengali, Arabic, Hindi or Chinese, are drawn as page images so the characters display correctly, and that text can't be selected.

Are line breaks and spacing kept?

Yes. Every line break and blank line is kept, and lines that are too long for the page wrap onto the next line. Tabs are converted to four spaces.

Which font should I choose?

Sans-serif or serif suit letters and notes. Monospace keeps columns of text aligned, which is best for code, logs and tables made with spaces.

Why do accented characters or symbols look wrong?

The tool reads UTF-8, UTF-16 and Windows-1252 text automatically. If a symbol can't be shown in the standard fonts, that file's pages are drawn as images instead, so characters are not replaced.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several TXT files, convert them together, and download each PDF or one ZIP.
