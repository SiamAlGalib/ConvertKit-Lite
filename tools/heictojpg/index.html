Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite HEIC to JPG tool.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

What is HEIC?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones use by default. It keeps good quality at a small size, but many apps, websites and older computers cannot open it. JPG works almost everywhere.

Will quality be lost?

JPG uses lossy compression, so a tiny amount of detail can be lost. Choose High (95%) to keep the result as close to the original as possible. JPG files are often larger than HEIC files at the same look.

Is photo information such as date and location kept?

No. The converted JPG contains the picture only, without EXIF details such as location. This is also handy when you want to share a photo without its location.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several HEIC files, convert them together, and download each JPG or one ZIP.

Why did a file fail to convert?

The file may be damaged, or it may use a HEIC variant your browser cannot decode. Very large photos can also run out of memory on phones, so try a smaller maximum size or fewer files at a time.
