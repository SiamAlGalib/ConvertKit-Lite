Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite Face Age Estimator.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Your photo is analyzed in your browser and is not uploaded to our server by this tool. The AI model files are downloaded from a public CDN the first time you estimate.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is approximate and meant for fun. Lighting, pose, image quality and model limitations can all change the result.

Why does it say no face was detected?

Try a clearer, front-facing photo with better lighting and no heavy filters or shadows covering the face.

Why does it say more than one face was found?

The estimator works on one person at a time. Crop the image so only one face is visible, then try again.

Why is the first estimate slower?

The AI model loads the first time you use it, so it needs an internet connection and a few extra seconds. Later estimates are faster.
