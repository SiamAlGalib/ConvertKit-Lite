Why convert WebP to JPG?

WebP is a modern image format that websites use because it makes small files. The trouble starts when you save one. A picture you download from a website often arrives as a .webp file that older apps, email programs, print shops, photo editors, document tools and some upload forms will not open.

JPG is the format almost everything accepts. This tool changes your WebP images into JPG on your own device, so your pictures are not uploaded anywhere.

How this tool converts your images

  • It decodes the WebP in your browser. The image is read on your device, so what it can open depends on your browser, and current browsers handle WebP.
  • It keeps the size in pixels. The JPG has the same width and height as the original. The result list shows the new dimensions.
  • It saves a JPG at your quality. Choose any value from 40% to 100%. The default is 90%.
  • It fills transparent areas. JPG cannot be see-through, so transparent parts become white, black or gray, whichever you pick.
  • It names the files for you. Each file keeps its name with a .jpg ending, and you can download them one by one or in a ZIP.

Choosing the right quality

  • 90%: the default and a good balance of sharpness and file size for most photos.
  • 100%: the best quality, with the biggest files. Pick this for pictures you plan to edit or print.
  • 60% to 75%: smaller files for email and websites. Fine detail, text and sharp edges may look a little softer.

JPG files are often bigger than the WebP they came from, because WebP is a more efficient format. That is normal. If the size matters, lower the quality, or use Image Compressor & Resizer afterwards.

Things to know

  • Transparency is lost. If you need a see-through background, keep the image as PNG instead. Image Converter can help with other formats.
  • Animated WebP becomes one picture. Only the first frame is converted, because JPG does not support animation.
  • Extra details are not kept. The image is redrawn and saved again, so information stored inside the file, such as camera details or location, is not carried over.
  • Only WebP files are accepted. Other types are skipped with a message.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite WebP to JPG tool.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens in your browser, so your images stay on your device.

Why convert WebP to JPG?

JPG opens almost everywhere, including older apps, email clients, print services and social platforms that do not accept WebP.

What happens to transparency?

JPG cannot store transparency. Transparent areas are filled with white, black or gray, whichever you choose. If you need transparency, keep the image as PNG instead.

Which quality should I choose?

90% is a good balance of sharpness and file size. Use 100% for the best quality, or go lower to get a smaller file.

What about animated WebP files?

Only the first frame is converted, because JPG does not support animation.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several WebP files, convert them together, and download each JPG or one ZIP.

Does the image size in pixels change?

No. The JPG has the same width and height as your WebP. Only the file format and quality change.

Why is my JPG bigger than the WebP?

WebP compresses images more efficiently than JPG, so the JPG is often larger at the same visual quality. Lower the quality slider, or run the JPG through the Image Compressor if you need it smaller.

Can I choose the background color for transparent areas?

Yes. Pick white, black or gray in the menu. White is the safest choice for most pictures, and black or gray can look better for white logos or light artwork.

Is the picture quality reduced?

Saving as JPG always compresses the image a little. At 90% or higher the difference is hard to see on most photos. Choose 100% for the best result.

Is photo information such as location kept?

No. The image is redrawn and saved as a new JPG, so details stored inside the file, such as camera settings or GPS location, are not carried over.

What will my files be called?

Each JPG keeps the name of the WebP with a .jpg ending. In the ZIP, files with the same name get a number added so nothing is overwritten.

Why was my file skipped?

Only .webp files are accepted. Other image types are skipped with a message. Use the matching converter for those formats.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. Very large images can take longer on older phones.

Is there a watermark or a limit?

No watermark, no account and no limit on how many images you convert.

Will converting WebP to JPG reduce quality?

Saving as JPG always compresses the image a little. At 90% or higher the difference is hard to see on most photos, and 100% gives the best result.

Why is my JPG bigger than the WebP?

WebP compresses images more efficiently than JPG, so the JPG is often larger at the same visual quality. Lower the quality slider if you need a smaller file.
