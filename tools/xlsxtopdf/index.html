Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite XLSX to PDF tool.

Are my spreadsheets uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is created in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Is the text in the PDF selectable?

Yes. The tool writes real text, not pictures, so you can select, copy and search it.

Which sheets are converted?

Every visible sheet in the workbook, in order, each starting on a new page. Hidden sheets and empty sheets are skipped.

What is not supported?

Charts, images, merged cells, cell colors, conditional formatting and exact Excel print settings. Non-Latin scripts such as Arabic, Chinese or Bengali appear as question marks.

Why do wide sheets look small?

Sheets with many columns are fitted to the page width by lowering the font size. Choose Landscape, or split the sheet in Excel, for a larger result.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several Excel files, convert them together, and download each PDF or one ZIP.
