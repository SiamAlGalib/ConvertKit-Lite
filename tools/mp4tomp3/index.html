Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about converting MP4 to MP3 with ConvertKit Lite.

Is it safe to convert my video here?

Yes. Conversion runs in your browser, so your file is not uploaded to a server.

Which file formats work?

MP4, MOV, M4A and WebM work in most browsers. Support depends on what your browser can decode, and Chrome handles the widest range.

Which MP3 quality should I choose?

192 kbps sounds good for most music and speech. Choose 320 kbps for the best quality, or 128 kbps for the smallest file.

Why did the conversion fail?

The video may have no audio track, use a format your browser can't decode, or be too large for your device's memory. Try a smaller file or Chrome.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no limit on our side. The whole file is decoded in memory, so videos longer than about an hour may fail on some devices.
