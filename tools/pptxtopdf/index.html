Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite PPTX to PDF tool.

Are my presentations uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is created in your browser, so your PPTX files stay on your device.

Will the PDF look exactly like PowerPoint?

It is a close copy for most slides. Text, shapes, colors and pictures are drawn using the slide layout. Charts, SmartArt, tables, gradients, animations and special effects may look different. For a pixel-perfect result, use Export as PDF inside PowerPoint.

Why does the text look different?

Fonts that are not installed on your device are replaced by a similar one, such as Arial. Spacing can shift slightly when that happens.

Can I select text in the PDF?

No. Each slide becomes a high-quality image on a PDF page, which keeps the design but makes the text non-selectable.

Can I convert password-protected or old .ppt files?

No. Only modern .pptx files are supported. Save older .ppt files as .pptx and remove any password in PowerPoint first.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add several PPTX files, convert them together, and download each PDF or one ZIP.
