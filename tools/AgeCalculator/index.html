Three quick steps. Nothing to install and nothing leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the ConvertKit Lite Age Calculator.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so your date of birth is not uploaded to a server.

Do I have to enter my birth time?

No, it is optional. Add it if you want your age in hours and minutes as well.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Calculate age until" date to any day after your birth date.

How are leap years handled?

The calculator uses real calendar dates, so leap years and different month lengths are taken into account.

Why does the time zone matter?

Results use your device's local date and time. If you were born in another time zone, hours and minutes may differ slightly.
